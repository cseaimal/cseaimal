## `whoami`

I'm **Aimal Khan**, a Computer Systems Engineering student at **UET Peshawar** working across hardware and software — embedded systems, secure IoT, and applied cryptography.

I'm an **Undergraduate Researcher at the Secured IoT Devices (SID) Lab**, currently building a **Post-Quantum Cryptography implementation on FPGA** — hardware-level cryptography designed to hold up against quantum-capable adversaries. Alongside research, I teach as a **STEAM Instructor** in the lab's outreach program.

I also lead outside the lab: I run the **AWS Student Builder Group** at UET Peshawar, captain the **Hiking & Trekking Club**, and lead **STEAM UP 2026**, a multi-day bootcamp bringing hands-on engineering to younger students.

The long-term goal is bigger than any one project: **Pakistan's first deep-tech IoT product company** — built on real infrastructure problems, not services work.
