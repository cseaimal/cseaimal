## About Me

I'm **Aimal Khan**, a Computer Systems Engineering student from **Peshawar, Pakistan**.

I work at the intersection of hardware and software — embedded systems, Flutter development, IoT, and cybersecurity. My focus isn't on collecting skills. It's on building systems that solve real problems.

Currently researching **Post-Quantum Cryptography on FPGA** and building smart embedded solutions for Pakistan's infrastructure gap.

I learn by shipping. I improve by understanding what I shipped.
