## `whoami`

<img align="right" src="https://media.giphy.com/media/qgQUggAC3Pfv687qPC/giphy.gif" width="270" />

I'm **Aimal Khan** — a Computer Systems Engineering student at **UET Peshawar**, working at the intersection of hardware and software: embedded systems, secure IoT, and Flutter development.

I'm an **Undergraduate Researcher at the Secured IoT Devices (SID) Lab**, currently researching **Post-Quantum Cryptography on FPGA** — building cryptographic hardware designed to withstand the next generation of computing threats. Alongside research, I teach as a **STEAM Instructor** for the SID Lab's outreach program.

Outside the lab, I lead people as much as I lead projects: I run the **AWS Student Builder Group** at UET Peshawar, captain the **Hiking & Trekking Club**, and serve as **Program Lead for STEAM UP 2026**, a multi-day bootcamp introducing young students to hands-on STEM.

My goal isn't a résumé of skills — it's **Pakistan's first deep-tech IoT product company**, built from the ground up on real infrastructure problems, not services work.
